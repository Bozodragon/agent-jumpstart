    After you press Enter, nothing may appear for up to half a minute. That wait is Windows PowerShell fetching the script, before our installer starts. Leave the window open.

    It asks a few questions, and pressing Enter picks the usual answer. When you need to sign in to something, it opens the page, says what to click and waits for you. It ends with a checklist of what it set up.

  2. Type /jumpstart in your top workspace

    Open T3 Code from the Start menu. Your top workspace, {{topWorkspace}}, is already in its list, because the installer added it. Start a new conversation there, type /jumpstart and press Enter.

    The agent gets to know you and your work, asks a few questions, shows you what it found so you can correct it, then sets up your other workspaces.

  3. Start work in the workspace it belongs to.

    Give each area of your work its own workspace, such as 'Event management' or 'Email campaigns'. Ask your agent to make one, for example 'Make me a workspace for event management'. Then open it in T3 Code and start a conversation there. Your messages, calendar and tasks go in Personal assistant.

When you need it

What Jumpstart does

When you type /jumpstart in your top workspace:

  • It asks a few questions: your job title and team, whether it may read your email, what you'd like help with, and how you like things written.
  • While you talk, a helper agent reads your calendar, Drive and Asana, and your email if you say yes. It also looks for you on the public web, such as a staff page. T3 Code shows it working.
  • It shows what it found on one page, the review page, for you to check and correct.
  • Nothing is kept until you've checked it. Then it writes your agent instructions and sets up your workspaces.

You can run /jumpstart again any time to update it.

Your workspaces

Your workspaces sit inside your top workspace, {{topWorkspace}}.

{{topWorkspace}}                your top workspace
├─ Personal assistant
├─ Thinking space
└─ a workspace for each area of your work

The top workspace's agent instructions apply in every workspace inside it. Instructions flow down, never across: what you set up in one workspace doesn't reach the ones beside it.

  • {{topWorkspace}}, your top workspace, is for setting things up: running /jumpstart, making a new workspace, and moving things over from claude.ai. It's not for everyday work.
  • Each area of your work, such as Event management or Email campaigns, has its own workspace. Each piece of work goes inside the workspace it belongs to.
  • Personal assistant is for organisation and admin: your messages, calendar and tasks.
  • Thinking space is for getting ideas out of your head, including thinking through something that might become a workspace of its own.

Making a new workspace. Ask your agent, in any workspace, for example "Make me a workspace for event management". For something narrower, it suggests the broader workspace it belongs in and puts the work there. Then open the new workspace in T3 Code and start a conversation there.

Your skills

A skill is a way of working your agent can follow. To use one, type / and its name, such as /grill-me. Most were written for software work: your agent follows their method and puts it in terms of your work. Each name links to the skill's own text.

Finding your way round T3 Code
ConversationsListed down the left, under each workspace. T3 Code calls them threads, and it calls workspaces "projects". Start a new one in the workspace the work belongs to. FilesThe files sidebar shows what's in the workspace you're in, such as its agent instructions. TerminalWhere you type a command, such as claude auth login. SettingsWhere you refresh your Claude sign-in and switch things on. Usage and contextHow much of your Claude allowance you've used, and how full this conversation is. When it's nearly full, start a new conversation. The context display starts switched off: turn it on in Settings, under Labs or Legacy depending on your version.

Claude sign-in didn't work

In T3 Code, go to Settings > Providers > Claude and click Refresh. Then type claude auth login in T3 Code's terminal, press Enter and follow what it says.

Asana shows the wrong workspace

When Asana asks, pick your team's workspace at the top of its page. To fix it, reconnect Asana as Your connectors says: disconnect it on claude.ai's Connectors page, connect it again and pick your team's workspace. Then start a new thread.
