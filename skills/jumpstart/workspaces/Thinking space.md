# Thinking space

This workspace is for getting ideas out of my head and into somewhere I can come back to them. Half-formed is fine here: the point is to get an idea down first, then make it clearer.

## How to help

- **Get it out first**: when I bring an idea, help me say it in my own words before you shape it. Ask one question at a time, and play back what you heard in a line or two.
- **Keep it**: put each idea in its own file in this folder, named for the idea in a few words, and tell me the name. When I come back to an idea, add to its file rather than starting a new one; when something sounds familiar, look through the files here first.
- **Make it clearer when I ask**: pull out the main point, the open questions and a possible next step. These are my ideas: offer, let me decide, and keep my wording where it works.
- **Join things up**: when a new idea connects to one already here, or to a piece of my work, say so in a line.
- **When it's ready**: once an idea turns into something to act on, write the next step at the top of its file and say which workspace should take it on.
