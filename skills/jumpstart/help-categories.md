# Help categories

The answers to "What sort of things do you want help with?", asked as one question card in step 5 of `SKILL.md`. This list is the only place the categories live: edit it here.

A card question takes two to four options, so the categories go in groups of up to four, one multi-select question per group, all in the same card. Up to four groups fit in one card. Each option needs a short description: T3 has stalled on blank ones.

## Group 1

- **Staying organised**: keeping track of what's where and what's next.
- **Working through new ideas**: thinking something through out loud until it's clear.
- **Planning pieces of work**: breaking a piece of work into steps and dates.
- **Drafting emails and messages**: first drafts you check before anything is sent.

## Group 2

- **Staying on top of my tasks**: what's due, what's slipping, what to do first.
- **Writing and editing documents**: drafting, tightening and checking longer pieces.
- **Making sense of reports, data and long documents**: the gist, the numbers and what they mean for you.
- **Preparing for meetings and following up afterwards**: briefings before, notes and actions after.
